Physical Models, Ray-Tracing Geometry & Adaptive Fusion Mathematics

1. Wi-Fi RF Bistatic & 4-Element ULA Model

Operating at carrier f = 5.8 GHz (λ ≈ 5.17 cm) with Tx at (0.5, 5.2) m and a 4-element vertical ULA Rx at (0.5, 0.8) m spaced by d = λ/2:

  • Bistatic Range: r(x,y) = ||p - p_Tx|| + ||p - p_Rx|| defines an ellipse with foci at Tx and Rx. Range resolution scales as Δr = c / (2B).
  • ULA Steering Vector: a_m(θ) = exp(j π m sin θ) for m = 0..3, where sin θ = (y - y_Rx)/||p - p_Rx||. Because linear arrays sense sin θ, reflections off the left wall (x = 0) create mirrored angle ghosts.

2. Acoustic 6-Element UCA 360° Beamforming

Positioned at (7.5, 5.2) m with radius R = 0.08 m and adjustable center frequency f_c ∈ [1, 4] kHz (c_s = 343 m/s):

  • Circular Manifold: a_m(φ) = exp(j (2π f_c / c_s) R cos(φ - 2πm/6)) resolves full 360° azimuth without front-back ambiguity, projecting a directional cone across the room.
  • Complementarity: Acoustic bearing cuts orthogonally across the RF bistatic ellipse, collapsing geometric dilution of precision (GDOP) near walls.

3. Exact 2D Image-Source Ray Tracing & Rack Diffraction

For all 8 planar reflectors (4 room walls with reflectivity Γ_wall and 4 faces of the metallic rack [3.4, 4.4] × [2.2, 3.8] m with Γ_rack = 0.96), the engine reflects source/receiver coordinates across each plane, validates finite-segment intersection, and tests segment-AABB rack occlusion. Occluded direct paths suffer knife-edge shadow attenuation L_diff (dB) and corner diffraction deflection, leaving reflected multipath ghosts dominant during NLoS shadowing.

4. Ground-Truth-Free Observable Adaptive Reliability Fusion

Without accessing the true target coordinates, each modality's reliability score s_m (m ∈ {RF, Ac}) is derived purely from observable sensor evidence:

1. Observable Effective SNR: SNR_eff = 10 log10(P_peak / P_median_floor)
2. Spectral Sharpness: q_sharp = (1 - H_norm(P)) · PSR_factor
3. Cross-Modal Consistency: ρ_BC = Σ_{x,y} sqrt( p_RF(x,y) · p_Ac(x,y) )
4. Tempered Log-Linear Pool: log P_adap(x,y) = w_RF log L_RF(x,y) + w_Ac log L_Ac(x,y) + log P_prior(x,y)

When an obstacle blocks one modality (lowering its SNR_eff and creating diffuse multipath peaks that reduce q_sharp), consistency-modulated softmax automatically shifts fusion weight toward the reliable modality, while P_prior(x,y) = 0 enforces physical non-penetration inside the solid metallic rack.
